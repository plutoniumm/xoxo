XOXO — Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe

Goff's quantum tic-tac-toe on a 3×3×3 grid. Marks live in superposition across two cells until a measurement forces them to a single cell.

How to play

  • On your turn, place one spooky mark superposed across two different cells. A link shows the two halves are entangled.
  • Cells fill up with overlapping spooky marks. When your move closes a loop of links, that cluster is measured: every mark in it collapses to one definite cell.
  • The collapse outcome is drawn from real quantum random numbers (Australia's ANU QRNG), pre-generated and bundled with the game.
  • Three definite marks in a row — along any line of the cube — wins.
